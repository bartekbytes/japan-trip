# Background

- I arrived to Miyajima around midday
- I stayed overnight - and I REALLY recommend this! (once all daily groups are gone it's even better vibe)

# Main shopping street / road next to the pier.

- Food options: stalls, restaurants: super big and cheap oysters and maple cookies that are made there in small batches.

# Shrine and Torii Gate

- Main attraction, both must visit.
- When low tide (afternoon), you can walk directly to the gate
- A lot of deers too over the island

# Daishoin

- Wow, that temple complex was great!
- I recommend to walk slowly, enjoy and see all places in this complex, including `Henjo Cave`

# Mount Misen

- There is a hiking route or cable car to the top
- I didn't go as the time I was on the Island a HUGE raid came and was not possible and worth going... Super regret I couldn't go
- You can check and consider this!
