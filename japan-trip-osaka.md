# Background

- I spent there 3 nighs in total, including day trips to `Nara`, `Uji`, `Kobe` and `Himeji`.
- So mostly I spent afternoons/evenings in Osaka
- Partying every day, to have some fun ;)

# Umeda Sky Building

Observation deck, great views over the city, if you're into this stuff...
Short walk from the main train station.

# Dotonbori

- The place to be! For food! And the best to go in the late afternoon/evening.
- A lot of these giant plastic food sculptures hanging on the restaurants.
- Also the place to eat fugu fish (the one if not done properly, can be lethal, as you can see, I survived hahaha)
- Famous Glico Running Man
- Just walk along the main street both sides of river/canal and some adjacent streets around, vibe in the afternoon/evening is amazing.

# Shinsekai / Tsutenkaku

- Another nice area to be, also with food, pachinko gambling
- If have time - go; if not much time - skip and focus on `Dotonbori`

# Osaka Castle

- Nice Japanese castle (you know, tall, multiple floors, all white)
- Of course the best is Himeji Castle and this is a blast! But I don't know if you plan Himeji Castle. If not Osaka one is also good.

# Side trips

`Osaka` was my place for daily trips.
I don't know if you're planning to go to any of the below.
Let me know, I can write more.

## Nara

- Deers! That bowing when you're giving them food :)
- Big park, really nice temples
- Great 1/2 day trip

## Himeji

- The biggest Japanese castle
- Impressive!
- You need to spend 1/2 easily
- Nice park next to it too

## Uji

- Tea place
- All about matcha, plenty of shops
- OK temple + one amazing one that my father and I discovered last time.
- Leisure, slow-pace 1/2 day

## Kobe

- Went there to `Sannomiya` area only for eating `Kobe beef`
- That was my the most expensive meal in my life, around 300 CHF (meat + japanese whisky)
- The experience of making the food in front of you by a chef is amazing, but...
- it's expensive ;)
